Calculation Model

Euler–Bernoulli Beam Theory

Bending stress:
σ = Mc / I

Maximum strain:
ε = σ / E

Beam equation:
EI · d²v/dx² = M(x)

E = Young's modulus
I = Second moment of area
M = Bending moment
c = Distance from neutral axis to outermost fiber

Assumptions: linear elastic material, small deformation, constant cross section and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory. Shear deformation is neglected.
